# Vendor Performance SLA Scorecard
**Document type:** Internal operations reference
**Review cycle:** Monthly
**Owner:** Operations Manager

---

## Overview

This scorecard defines the performance metrics used to evaluate logistics vendors and carriers. Scores are reviewed monthly. A vendor scoring below 3.0 overall enters a corrective action review. A vendor scoring below 2.0 for two consecutive months is placed on the vendor suspension list.

**Scoring scale:**
- 5 = Constantly exceeds expectations
- 4 = Meets and exceeds expectations
- 3 = Meets expectations
- 2 = Requires improvement or additional assistance
- 1 = Failing — corrective action plan required within 5 working days

## SLA breach thresholds and escalation

| Overall score | Action |
|---|---|
| 4.0 – 5.0 | Preferred vendor; eligible for contract renewal at current rates |
| 3.0 – 3.9 | Satisfactory; standard review at contract renewal |
| 2.5 – 2.9 | Performance notice issued; improvement plan required within 10 working days |
| 2.0 – 2.4 | Formal corrective action plan required within 5 working days; monthly review |
| Below 2.0 | Vendor suspension review; contract termination if score not above 2.5 within 60 days |

---

## SLA breach notification procedure

When a vendor misses a metric that drops the monthly score below 3.0:

1. Operations Manager issues a written exception notice within 2 working days of month-end
2. Vendor must acknowledge within 24 hours
3. Corrective action plan must be submitted within 5 working days
4. Follow-up review scheduled for 30 days after plan submission
5. If metric is not restored, contract review meeting initiated with senior management

---

## Notes on customs-related metrics

For vendors managing customs clearance:

- **FASAH submission on time** (24 hours before vessel arrival): scored under Section 3 compliance
- **Customs hold rate due to vendor error** (incorrect declaration, wrong HS code): scored under Section 4 service quality; any hold caused by vendor error that results in a delay of more than 24 hours is flagged as a major exception regardless of the overall score
- Holds caused by incomplete client documentation are excluded from vendor scoring
