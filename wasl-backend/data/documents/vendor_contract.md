# Vendor Logistics Services Contract
**Contract Reference:** WLS-2024-KSA-007
**Date:** 1 January 2024
**Parties:**
- **Service Provider:** Al-Wasl Freight Solutions Co. Ltd., CR No. 1010456789, Riyadh, KSA
- **Client:** Arabian Consumer Goods Distribution LLC, CR No. 4030112233, Jeddah, KSA

---

## 1. Scope of services

The Service Provider agrees to provide the Client with the following logistics services within the Kingdom of Saudi Arabia:

- Road freight transportation between all major KSA cities (Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam, Makkah, Madinah)
- Last-mile delivery to retail and commercial consignees
- Warehousing and cross-docking at the Service Provider's Riyadh hub
- Customs clearance coordination for international shipments via FASAH
- Cold-chain logistics for temperature-sensitive goods (2–8°C and -18°C ranges)

---

## 2. Service Level Agreement (SLA)

### 2.1 Delivery timelines

| Route | Standard Delivery | Express Delivery |
|---|---|---|
| Riyadh–Jeddah | 48 hours | 24 hours |
| Riyadh–Dammam | 24 hours | 12 hours |
| Riyadh–Makkah/Madinah | 48 hours | 24 hours |
| Inter-city (major cities) | 72 hours | 48 hours |
| Remote locations | 5 working days | 72 hours |

Timelines are measured from the time goods are accepted at the Service Provider's hub with complete documentation. They exclude Saudi public holidays as declared by the Supreme Judicial Council.

### 2.2 Performance metrics

| KPI | Minimum standard | Target |
|---|---|---|
| On-time delivery rate | 95% | 98% |
| Order accuracy (correct items, quantities) | 99.5% | 99.9% |
| Shipment damage rate | < 0.5% | < 0.1% |
| Exception notification (delay alert to client) | Within 4 hours of exception | Within 2 hours |
| Customer delivery attempt (first attempt) | Within SLA window | Day before deadline |
| Proof of delivery (POD) upload | Within 24 hours of delivery | Same day |
| Lost shipment rate | < 0.1% | Zero |

### 2.3 Customs clearance SLA

For international shipments requiring customs clearance:

- The Service Provider will submit the customs declaration through FASAH within **24 hours** of receiving the complete document set from the Client
- The Client must provide all required documents (commercial invoice with CR number, certificate of origin, bill of lading, packing list, and any required approvals) at least **48 hours before the vessel/aircraft arrival**
- If documents are received after the 48-hour window, the SLA clock resets to 24 hours from document receipt
- **Customs holds due to missing or incorrect documentation provided by the Client do not count against the Service Provider's SLA**
- Customs holds due to errors in the Service Provider's declaration submissions are the Service Provider's responsibility and the SLA continues to run

## 3. Exception handling and notifications

### 3.1 Exception categories

| Category | Definition | Response requirement |
|---|---|---|
| **Minor delay** | Delivery will be late by 1–12 hours | Notify client within 4 hours of identifying delay |
| **Major delay** | Delivery will be late by more than 12 hours | Notify client within 2 hours; provide revised ETA |
| **Customs hold** | Shipment held at port by ZATCA | Notify client within 1 hour of hold notification; state reason |
| **Lost shipment** | Shipment not located after 48 hours | Notify client immediately; initiate investigation |
| **Damaged goods** | Damage identified at any point | Photograph evidence within 1 hour; notify client immediately |
| **Failed delivery** | Consignee unavailable or address incorrect | Notify client within 2 hours; await re-delivery instruction |

### 3.2 Re-delivery policy

- First delivery attempt: included in service fee
- Second delivery attempt: included in service fee
- Third and subsequent attempts: charged at 50% of standard delivery rate per attempt
- Goods held awaiting re-delivery instructions: free for 48 hours; SAR 15/day per shipment thereafter
- Return to origin after 3 failed attempts: charged at standard delivery rate

---

## 4. SLA breach and penalties

### 4.1 Delivery delays

| Delay period beyond SLA | Penalty |
|---|---|
| 1–12 hours | 5% credit on the shipment invoice |
| 12–24 hours | 10% credit on the shipment invoice |
| 24–48 hours | 15% credit on the shipment invoice |
| More than 48 hours | 25% credit on the shipment invoice; Client may cancel without penalty |

### 4.2 Performance metric breach

If the Service Provider's on-time delivery rate falls below **93%** in any calendar month, the Client may:
- Issue a formal written notice requiring a corrective action plan within 5 working days
- Apply a 3% reduction to all invoices for that month
- Terminate the contract with 30 days notice if performance remains below 93% for two consecutive months

### 4.3 Exclusions from SLA

The following are excluded from the SLA and no penalties apply:

- Saudi public holidays as officially announced
- Acts of God, natural disasters, or force majeure events
- Customs holds caused by Client documentation errors or missing prior approvals
- Delays caused by consignee unavailability or incorrect address provided by Client
- Border closures or government restrictions

---

## 5. Liability

- Maximum liability per shipment: SAR 50,000 or the declared value of the goods, whichever is lower
- Claims must be submitted in writing within 7 days of the delivery date or expected delivery date
- The Service Provider maintains cargo insurance covering all goods in transit up to SAR 500,000 per vehicle

---

## 6. Contract term and termination

- Initial term: 12 months from 1 January 2024
- Automatic renewal for 12-month periods unless either party gives 60 days written notice
- Immediate termination by either party in the event of material breach unremedied within 15 days of written notice
